🔍 Tomato Egg Rice
⏱ 10 min  ·  ⭐ Easy  ·  🌾 Asian
~350
kcal

Protein
42g

Ingredients
🍅Tomato×1 large
🥚Eggs×2
🍚Leftover rice1 cup
🧂Soy sauce1 tbsp
🛢️Sesame oil1 tsp
🧅Spring onionfor topping
Steps
1
Scramble eggs
Beat eggs with a pinch of salt. Cook on medium heat until just set, breaking into pieces.
2
Sauté tomatoes
Add diced tomatoes to the same pan. Cook until they soften and release juice.
3
Add rice
Mix in leftover rice and stir-fry until combined and heated through.
4
Season
Add soy sauce and sesame oil. Toss everything together.
5
Serve
Plate and top with chopped spring onion. Serve immediately.
